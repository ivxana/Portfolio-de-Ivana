YUP — Youth United Project

Expansions Officer

Overview

Bringing free tutoring to newcomer families across the GTA.

YUP is a nonprofit providing free virtual tutoring to 500+ students across Canada.

What I Do

  • Lead enrollment growth toward 1,000+ students, focused on newcomer and immigrant families
  • Extend YUP’s outreach beyond its existing Chinese communities
  • Build the sponsorship pipeline funding YUP’s Volunteer of the Month program
  • Track enrollment sources and sponsorship metrics to guide outreach

Why It Matters to Me

Educational equity is one of the things I care about most. Free tutoring for the kids who need it is the kind of direct impact that changes trajectories.

The organization is already strong within Chinese-Canadian communities through its Mandarin-language channels. I want to extend that same access to the other immigrant communities across Halton and the GTA who aren’t currently being reached. Access shouldn’t stop at a language boundary. My job is to make sure it doesn’t.
