Vote16 Canada

Volunteer

Overview

Making the case for lowering the federal voting age.

Vote16 Canada is a national campaign to extend federal voting rights to 16- and 17-year-olds. I’m advocating right alongside them.

Why It Matters to Me

If Tallied is my product answer to democratic inclusion, Vote16 is the policy version.

Young people are affected by every policy the government passes. We contribute to funding the government. We’re informed. We show up. But we don’t get to vote on any of it - and I don’t think that holds up.

Lowering the voting age isn’t hypothetical. It already works. Sixteen-year-olds have voted in Métis Nation elections and municipal participatory budgeting in cities across Canada, and the sky didn’t fall.
